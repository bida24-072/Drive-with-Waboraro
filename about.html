Hi, I'm Waboraro

Driving theory coach. TikTok teacher. Motswana, from Lobatse.

I teach driving theory the way it should be taught.

I started teaching driving theory because I saw too many people fail — not because they weren't smart, but because nobody had explained things simply. Books felt like homework. Videos were boring. Teachers moved too fast.

So I started making my own lessons. Short. Clear. Friendly. Free.

What began as a few TikTok videos has become a proper way of learning — over 1,200 students taught, and a 95% pass rate.

My students come from Lobatse, Gaborone, Kanye, Maun, and beyond. Some are teenagers getting their first licence. Others are adults who've been putting off the test for years.

Whoever you are — welcome. Let's get you through this.

— Waboraro

My teaching philosophy

Learning is not about memorising. It's about understanding.

When you understand why a rule exists — why you must stop at a red octagon, why you signal before turning — you never forget it. That's my approach. Every lesson explains not just what, but why.

I use plain language. I use real Botswana examples. I use pictures, stories, and repetition. And I never make a student feel stupid for asking questions.

If you're ready to pass — I'm ready to teach.
